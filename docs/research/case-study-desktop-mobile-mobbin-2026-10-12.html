A direction for Moriz

Show the work.
Explain the choices.

Koto provides the closest studio reference. Combine its focus on project imagery with Attio’s compact synopsis, Nite Riot’s explicit contribution credits, and an editorial narrative supported by real screens and an attributed client quote.

01 / Opening + overview

Project name, one clear sentence, strong image, client, year and studio role. Keep the route back to all projects visible.

02 / Challenge

Who needed what, the starting problem and the constraints. Keep the explanation specific to this project.

03 / Decisions + screens

Two or three consequential choices, each explained beside a real interface or comparison.

04 / Delivered solution

Readable screens, concise captions and responsive galleries. Video should support the story.

05 / Outcome + credit

What shipped, documented results and approved client feedback. Name collaborators and the studio’s actual contribution.

06 / Continue

Next project, all projects and a WhatsApp enquiry about a similar project.

For Hebrew mobile pages, use one reading column, generous Leon body type, readable interface images and a compact overview. Keep essential narrative open and prevent chapter navigation from competing with the existing header.

Evidence and limits

This is visual precedent research. It does not test conversion, accessibility, performance or complete interaction behavior. The three creative/production projects are direct portfolio references; the sixteen customer stories supply adjacent narrative patterns. Sierra adds a design-team interview study.

Retired or redirected source pages were excluded. Recommendations are design judgments, separate from screenshot observations.
