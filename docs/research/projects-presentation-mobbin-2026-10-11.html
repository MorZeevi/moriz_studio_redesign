Apply the patterns to Moriz

The CMS already supports separate covers for Home and the case page, plus ordered text, images, captions, video and galleries. The initial trial can focus on content and image selection.

  1. Home: keep a recognizable interface cover, a one-sentence explanation, the studio’s services and a visible case study action. Keep all projects discoverable through vertical scrolling.
  2. Case study: explain the challenge, then two or three decisions with real supporting screens. Keep the main narrative open; reserve disclosures for optional detail.
  3. Evidence: document what shipped. Add usage figures or a client quote only with a source, context and publication permission.
  4. Mobile and Hebrew: stack overview, cover and story in a useful reading order. Keep screen details legible and review the space occupied by accumulating homepage headers. These are proposed adaptations, not new mobile test findings.
  5. Continue browsing: consider next-project and collection links at the end of the story. These would require a later UI change; the current detail page links back to Home.

Evidence and limits

11 Mobbin searches returned 46 placements representing 40 unique references. All returned previews were examined; 14 full-resolution assets were retained locally. This is a visual precedent study, not customer research or usability testing.

Retrieval date is not capture date. Still images do not verify motion, sticky behavior, accessibility, performance or conversion. This board contains sections and modules, not 14 complete case studies.
