# Footer contact form — Mobbin research

Research date: 12 October 2026. Purpose: a gentle, classic, minimal enquiry form inside Moriz Studio’s homepage footer.

## Selected direction

An open invitation on the right and a quiet form on the left at desktop widths; invitation first, then one column of fields on mobile. Retain Moriz’s Leon typography, rounded footer, light/dark palette and animated wordmark. Thin field rules and a compact outlined button make the task clear without adding a card.

### [Savor](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ae1f5fe6-9420-45e4-b097-64ae2aa8c06e)

An open two-column composition with restrained copy, generous space, thin rules beneath the fields and a small send action. Borrow the quiet rhythm and field treatment; retain Moriz typography and omit the category dropdown.

[Saved full-resolution reference](assets/footer-contact-2026-10-12/savor.webp)

### [Rains](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/82ce6566-c33e-4b16-8750-670aa04be011)

A sparse contact page with underlined text fields, a distinct message area and an outlined send button. Borrow the restrained controls; use persistent labels instead of placeholder-only instructions.

[Saved full-resolution reference](assets/footer-contact-2026-10-12/rains.webp)

### [Assembly Coffee](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/59b6274b-06a1-46f0-825f-3bc2b0db9d19)

An invitation and explanatory paragraph sit beside a compact enquiry form. Borrow the readable separation between invitation and task; keep one full-name field and use rules rather than filled input boxes.

[Saved full-resolution reference](assets/footer-contact-2026-10-12/assembly-coffee.webp)

## Decisions implemented

- Heading: **בואו נדבר**. Invitation: **ספרו לי בקצרה על הפרויקט שלכם.**
- Fields: full name, optional phone, email and a short message. Name and phone share the desktop row; email and message span the form.
- Name, email and message are required. Persistent labels, Hebrew inline errors, keyboard focus, correct input types and autocomplete support the task.
- The former standalone contact placeholder is removed. One `#contact` target now lives at the top of the footer.
- The owner selected design and validation only for this pass. The preview notice is always visible; valid submissions retain input and say that nothing was sent. No request, email, WhatsApp handoff or storage is triggered.
- Existing links, social/legal placeholders, wordmark motion and floating controls remain.

## Method and limits

Two Mobbin website-section searches returned eight placements / seven unique references. Every preview was inspected. Three references were retained and downloaded from Mobbin’s full-resolution image URLs, rather than the inline previews. The searches and all result metadata are recorded in the JSON manifest. These are composition references; no claim is made about their validation behavior or conversion performance.
