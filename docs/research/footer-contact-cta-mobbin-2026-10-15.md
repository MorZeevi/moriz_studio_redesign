# Footer contact CTA — Mobbin research

Date: 15 October 2026. Scope: a short contact form inside the site's existing shared footer. References were inspected through Mobbin and their full-resolution section images were saved locally for design review. The images are research evidence, not site assets.

| Reference | Useful pattern | Local image |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Oevra contact section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/a8630ac7-59ae-4bc4-b348-fc9c61dcdc6b) | Open two-column layout: a prominent invitation beside a compact form with thin field rules. This is the primary composition reference. | [oevra.webp](assets/footer-contact-cta-2026-10-15/oevra.webp) |
| [Titan Intake contact section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/7f4a9216-5bf5-4c00-8ca9-9edd38bd9a43) | A full-width colored contact surface gives the form a clear closing-CTA role and keeps the action legible. | [titan-intake.webp](assets/footer-contact-cta-2026-10-15/titan-intake.webp) |
| [YLLW contact section](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/a45b58fa-4ac0-49f8-957e-47be93d4f4e1) | Large contact typography. Its long form was not adopted because this site's agreed handoff needs only name, phone, channel preference and optional message. | [yllw.webp](assets/footer-contact-cta-2026-10-15/yllw.webp) |

Initial decision: use Oevra's open invitation/form balance and ruled controls on the site's CTA-red surface, with the existing Noise texture. Keep the form short and the submit button visually stable as the WhatsApp checkbox changes the external handoff destination. The footer navigation and wordmark remain below on the theme surface.

Owner refinement, 15 October: the [supplied liquid-button shape](assets/footer-contact-cta-2026-10-15/owner-liquid-shape.png) is the visual authority for the contact panel. The later [annotated contact screenshot](assets/footer-contact-cta-2026-10-15/owner-contact-join.png) calls for a grainy CTA-red inner shell on the regular theme canvas, with much deeper top and bottom curves at the join and button-like liquid motion. Keep the right-hand invitation, left-hand form, two-column balance and ruled fields. Stack the same content in a simple rounded shape on mobile. This supersedes the earlier light panel on a red band.

Final join correction, 15 October: the owner compared the actual small button with the oversized panel and rejected the deep hourglass curves. Keep the red grain panel and liquid motion, but scale the button's short, shallow top and bottom notches into the large two-lobe join. The button is the geometry reference; the annotated contact screenshot remains the color and layout reference.
