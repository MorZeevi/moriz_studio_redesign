This report uses Mobbin website sections to investigate positioning, capabilities, portfolio presentation, case studies, process, trust, FAQ and contact. The intended audience is Moriz’s prospective clients at existing businesses and startups, especially product managers with a clear web-system need.

The recommendations below are proposals for content and layout review. Moriz’s established homepage sequence, supplied copy, approved foundations and floating-only WhatsApp access remain the project context.

The strongest takeaways

Give visuals a job. The selected Fiasco work grid combines images with project descriptions. Koto’s case-study capture supplies a challenge alongside the work. For Moriz, the useful content is what was built, who it serves and what the owner contributed.

Explain the combined offering. Locomotive groups capabilities, while Aino explicitly names engineering. Moriz’s research, experience and development themes can carry the same clarity using its own confirmed scope and plain language.

Make the first commitment understandable. Ragged Edge’s numbered process is a useful presentation reference. Moriz’s section should explain its actual starting steps: a 5–10 minute call, a specification discussion and a quote.

Use attributable evidence. Büro’s testimonial keeps speaker details visible; Twenty’s customer story groups outcomes and attribution. For Moriz, source-backed usage evidence and real quotes are the material to collect next.

These are design hypotheses based on a selected screenshot sample. They are not measured conversion or usability findings.

Method, exclusions and open evidence

Twelve focused Mobbin section searches returned 39 results, representing 36 unique section IDs. Every returned preview was visually inspected. Fourteen readable, useful captures were selected, balancing direct agency/studio references with explicitly labeled adjacent examples.

Search labels were treated as retrieval prompts, not proof of the screenshot’s content or page position. Several “hero” results were visibly service introductions; the report classifies them by what the capture actually shows. Section screenshots cannot establish complete homepage order.

  • Excluded captures: Trawelt and one Büro FAQ result were largely blank. Phantom Studios and Analogue’s testimonial result showed obscured or partial content.
  • Wrong visitor intent: Analogue’s FAQ concerns careers and workplace culture. Koto’s returned contact section invites applications. Neither supports a client-inquiry recommendation.
  • Date limits: 9 October 2026 is the research date. Mobbin did not supply capture or launch dates; no claim is made that these websites were launched or captured in 2026.
  • Behavior limits: no live-site interaction, mobile layout, accessibility, speed or conversion testing was performed. A visible accordion or media control does not verify its behavior.
  • Evidence to collect: real project visuals; accurate contribution and engagement context; actual usage figures with source and timeframe; selected testimonials and attribution; recurring client questions; confirmed Contact/Footer content.
