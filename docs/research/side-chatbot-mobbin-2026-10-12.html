Now

A 22rem box headed “לא מצאת תשובה?”: one question, one answer. Each new question replaces the last answer. No avatar, no suggestions, no motion.

Proposed

  1. A full-height drawer on the pills' edge. Non-modal on desktop, full screen on phones.
  2. Mor's photo on the launcher (“Ask moriz.ai”), in the header and beside each answer.
  3. A greeting and four suggested questions. The placeholder types “אפשר לשאול כל שאלה…”.
  4. A real thread that remembers context, with a thinking bubble and a word-by-word reveal.
  5. A slow red-and-yellow gradient on the input ring and the empty state.

HoneyBook

AI assistant docked over the dashboard. · WEB

Visible patternA rounded, nearly full-height panel floats over the right edge with a small gap. It has a header with history, expand and close icons, a long answer, feedback icons and the composer pinned at the bottom. The page behind stays visible.

Adaptation for MorizThe core drawer shape: inset from the viewport, rounded, non-modal, with the composer pinned to the bottom.

CautionIt is dense for a marketing site. Keep the header to identity and close.

Apply to Moriz

  1. Drawer, not popover. About 26rem wide and full height, inset 12px, rounded, with the composer pinned to the bottom (HoneyBook, HubSpot).
  2. Face first. Mor's portrait with a turning gradient ring in the header; 28px avatars on answers only (Delphi, Aboard, Resend).
  3. Empty state. Greeting, a one-line scope, and four questions built from the live knowledge rows (Resend, Origin, Otter).
  4. Progress you can see. Echo the question, show thinking dots with the avatar, then reveal the answer word by word (Resend).
  5. Gentle colour. A thin conic ring on the composer and slow low-opacity blobs, all switched off under reduced motion (Jobber, Cleo).

Method

Five Mobbin searches (four web, one iOS) returned 54 placements. Every preview was reviewed and 15 were kept. See the manifest for exact queries, IDs and exclusions.

Limits

Still images don't show slide-in motion, typing or gradient movement, so those recommendations are design judgment. No usability testing was done. The first-person voice depends on a prompt change that is still pending approval.
