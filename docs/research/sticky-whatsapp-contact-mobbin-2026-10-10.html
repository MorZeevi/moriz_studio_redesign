Start with these three

Mailchimp — labeled pill. The closest reference for adding a clear invitation beside the current WhatsApp icon. Retain the green background, charcoal mark and generous padding.

ExpressVPN — edge tab. A smaller text-led alternative to a circle. Compare its unobtrusive placement with the more visible pill.

Kiwi.com — bottom bar. Study its single-action layout for a possible mobile contact bar. This would be an adaptation of a desktop booking screen, not a mobile WhatsApp example.

My recommendation is to compare the labeled green pill against the current circle first. A full-width mobile bar is a second experiment if stronger visibility is worth the space it occupies. These are design hypotheses, not conversion findings.

What the references establish: visible controls and their composition. Screenshots cannot establish persistence on scroll, responsive behavior, response time or conversion performance.

Most examples use live chat or booking actions. Base44 shows an explicit WhatsApp handoff, inside a static connection card. No exact sticky WhatsApp contact bar was confirmed in this search.

The dark “curated by Mobbin” strip at the bottom of each image is a watermark, not the website’s contact bar. Screenshots are saved locally at 1920 × 1320, rather than embedded through expiring preview links.

Search method and exclusions

Searched Mobbin website sections and web application screens on 10 October 2026. Reviewed all 54 returned previews, representing 49 unique candidates, and retained nine. The search used these descriptions:

  1. A floating WhatsApp contact button or WhatsApp chat bar pinned to the bottom corner of a website
  2. A floating contact bar at the bottom of a website with a contact us button and a short invitation to start a project
  3. A website with a sticky bottom bar containing contact, chat or message actions
  4. A website landing page with a small floating live chat support launcher button in the bottom right corner
  5. A floating pill navigation bar centered at the bottom of a website with a contact button
  6. A product details page with a floating bottom call to action bar containing a primary booking or contact button
  7. A public website page with a floating customer support chat launcher button or chat with us tab at the bottom right corner
  8. A website hero with a floating bottom navigation dock and a let's talk contact call to action
  9. A compact live chat tab labeled chat with us or help at the bottom edge of a website
  10. A customer support page with a button to contact the business on WhatsApp

Large footer CTAs, inbox composers and brand pages with no useful contact control were excluded from the shortlist. Expanded support panels are retained only as additional states, with their limitations labeled.

Every recommendation above is an interpretation for Moriz. Existing WhatsApp behavior and the confirmed destination remain in place; no site redesign is selected or implemented by this research.
