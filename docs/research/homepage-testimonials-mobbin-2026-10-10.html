Three directions to compare

Recommended · implemented as a local trial

Spotlight + client index

Büro attribution + V7/HoneyBook navigation + Biograph scale + Attio identity column

Desktop (64rem and wider): a ruled client index on the right in RTL, with number, 48px avatar, name and role, works as a keyboard-operable tablist. A counter and arrows sit below it. The spotlight on the left shows a quote mark in the brand-red accent, a Danidin headline when supplied, and Leon body text clamped to six lines behind להמשך קריאה. Panels are stacked in one cell for a stable height. Switching uses a crossfade with no autoplay, and it is instant under reduced motion. Without JavaScript, all quotes are shown.

On mobile: Horizontal avatar chips → quote → read more → attribution → counter and arrows.

Tradeoff: One quote is visible at a time. Short quotes leave space beneath them because the height follows the tallest clamped panel. Elementor and Shay Goren have no headline yet.

Alternative: everything visible

Büro stack

Büro testimonial + Campsite rules

Every testimonial listed vertically: an attribution column and a large quote per row, separated by fine rules like the Projects section. No interaction.

On mobile: Attribution → full quote for each row, with no controls.

Tradeoff: With four or five testimonials, including one of about 150 words, the section becomes very long before Getting Started and FAQ.

Rejected for now

Equal card grid

Campsite 2×2 grid, Cursor 3×2 cards

Equal cards in a ruled grid, each with an icon or avatar, a quote and a footer.

On mobile: One card per row, which is effectively the stack with card chrome.

Tradeoff: Quotes range from about 30 to about 150 words, so cards either leave large empty areas or truncate the longest quote.

Large quote with a separate attribution column

Strong layout reference
Visible in the screenshot
A fine rule runs across the top. A small name, organization and role sit in a narrow column on the left; a large two-paragraph quote fills the right half. Only one quote is visible, with no controls in the crop.
For Moriz

Navigation reference

Search coverage and source limits

Three Mobbin section searches (page 1 each, with further pages available) returned 34 results naming 31 distinct sites. Clay appears three times and Leonardo AI twice. Only the eight images shown here were inspected for this record. The remaining results were not promoted, and their individual reasons were not recorded.

Live source sites were not checked; Büro notes are carried over from the earlier record. 10 October 2026 is the research and download date, not a verified capture or redesign date.
