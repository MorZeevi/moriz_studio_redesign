Three directions to compare

Recommended · implemented

Hairline columns on a rail

Runway, Mora, Wild, monday.com

Desktop (64rem and wider): three columns separated by fine vertical rules, with a 1px rail across the top and a node where each column starts. Each step has a two-tone icon in a ruled tile, its number, a short title, one line of description and quiet chips for the fine print; “ללא עלות” is the only emphasised chip. As the section enters the viewport the rail fills from right to left in RTL, each node lights as the line reaches it, the step text rises in, and each icon plays once: the phone rings, the Zoom “live” dot blinks, the quote’s check draws in.

On mobile: The rail runs vertically along the right edge with a node beside each icon; steps stack and each reveals when it reaches the screen.

Tradeoff: Adds a client component and a small amount of motion. The rail implies a timeline, so the steps must stay strictly sequential.

Alternative

Editorial ruled rows

United Carriers

Full-width rows separated by rules: number, icon and large title on the start side, the description and chips on the end side.

On mobile: Each row stacks: number and title, then description and chips.

Tradeoff: The most studio-like and the simplest to build, but it reads as a list rather than a sequence, and three rows look sparse.

Rejected

Equal cards

Cloudstudio, Mews, Wise

Three bordered or tinted cards with an icon or image, a number, a title, a line and chips.

On mobile: One card per row.

Tradeoff: The most generic option. Card backgrounds add weight that the rest of the ruled homepage avoids, and image cards need product imagery Moriz does not have.

The three steps, as implemented

  1. שיחת טלפון קצרהכמה שאלות פשוטות, כדי לראות אם יש התאמה ראשונית לצרכים שלכם.5–10 דקות
  2. שיחת אפיוןנכיר לעומק את הפרויקט, המטרות והצרכים שלכם.ללא עלותבזום
  3. קבלת הצעת מחירהצעה מסודרת שמבוססת על מה שאפיינו יחד.בתוקף ל־14 ימי עבודה

The facts are owner-supplied: a short phone call with a few simple questions to check initial fit, a discovery call on Zoom free of charge, and a quote valid for 14 working days. The lines for steps 2 and 3 and the shortened step 2 title are drafts awaiting approval. Icons: the existing mobile.svg plus new video.svg and quote.svg in public/icons/, inlined in src/components/getting-started-icons.tsx so they follow the theme and animate.

The source screenshots are LTR, so their layouts are mirrored for RTL: the rail starts on the right.

01

Runway

Three steps on a partly filled progress line, with a meta row under each

Strong layout reference
Visible in the screenshot
Heading “Fast track to clarity” with a short intro on the right. A white rounded panel holds “Onboarding guided by finance experts” and an orange “Book a demo” button. A thin line runs across with a dot above each of three columns; the line is dark up to the first column’s end and pale after it. Each column has a boxed chip (“01 | Kickoff”), a bold one-line title, two lines of description and a grey meta line: “1-week average”, “4-week average”, “Ongoing”.
For Moriz
The direct model for the rail and the meta row. Moriz moves its fine print (free of charge, Zoom, valid for 14 working days) into quiet chips in the same position, so the one-line description stays about what happens. In RTL the rail fills from the right.
Tradeoff
Runway wraps the steps in a card panel with a CTA inside; Moriz keeps the open hairline layout and adds no CTA, because WhatsApp stays floating. The static capture does not show whether the line animates.

Marketing-page context with site navigation and a rating badge. Original capture page unconfirmed; live site not checked.

02

Mora

Hairline columns with boxed numbers; two of the three steps are calls

Strong layout reference
Visible in the screenshot
A two-tone heading (“Don’t have time? We’ll set it up for you”) on the left; an intro paragraph and a “Book demo” pill with two small avatars on the right. Below, three columns separated by vertical hairlines and dashed grid guides, each with a tiny boxed number (“01”), a short title (“Onboarding Call”, “Workspace Setup”, “Check-in Call”) and two lines of description. The timing (“You’ll be live in less than two weeks”) sits in the intro.
For Moriz
The base of the Moriz layout: no card backgrounds, fine rules between columns, small numbers. Its content, with calls as steps, is the closest match to Moriz’s phone call → Zoom call → quote.
Tradeoff
Very quiet: without a rail or icons the steps read like a feature list. Fine print in the intro paragraph is easy to miss, which is why Moriz attaches it to each step as chips.

Section context only. Original capture page unconfirmed; live site not checked.

03

Wild

Phase timeline with square nodes and mono meta labels

Rail and meta reference
Visible in the screenshot
Heading “We build it with you.” over a paragraph that states the duration (“about 14 weeks, in four phases”). Four columns sit on a thin horizontal line; each starts with a small square node in its own colour, then a mono label (“PHASE 1 · WKS 1–4”), a title and two to three lines of description. A faint grid texture covers the background.
For Moriz
Shows that a node on a line plus a small meta label can carry timing without crowding the description. Supports Moriz’s node-per-step rail and the quiet chips.
Tradeoff
It describes a four-phase delivery process, not client onboarding. A different colour per node would compete with Moriz’s single brand-red accent, so Moriz uses one accent for every node.

Section context only. Original capture page unconfirmed; live site not checked.

Before accepting the section

Review the section visually on desktop and mobile, in light and dark, then confirm the remaining content.

  • Approval or rewording of the drafted lines for steps 2 and 3, and of the shortened step 2 title (“שיחת אפיון” instead of “שיחת אפיון מעמיקה”).
  • Whether the phone call is still 5–10 minutes (carried over from the earlier wireframe copy).
  • Optional: what the client should prepare before the Zoom call. No Zoom duration is shown (owner decision).
Search coverage and source limits

Six Mobbin section searches (page 1 each, limit 8, with further pages available) returned 48 results: 39 distinct sections from 36 distinct sites. Section IDs were retained for every result. Eight selected images were downloaded as WebP at 1920 pixels wide with no failures. Canonical Mobbin links are kept in the source log.

Only the eight images shown here were inspected at full size. Rejected patterns were assessed from previews: sticky split or stacked scroll cards (Framer, Kalstore, Air, Fiasco); accordion of steps (Titan); company-history timelines (Circle, SpaceXAI, Grok, Square, Apollo, Framer); illustrated centred columns (Blue Apron, ClassPass, HubSpot, Duolingo, Amie, Visitors). The remaining results (Grammarly, Escape Cafe, Kastle, PayPal, Ragged Edge, Parker AI, FLORA, V7, YLLW, Maxima Therapy, Granola, Adaline) were not promoted, and their individual reasons were not recorded.

Live source sites were not checked. 10 October 2026 is the research and download date, not a verified capture or redesign date. Static screenshots do not establish animation or scroll behaviour. Mobile descriptions are proposed Moriz adaptations.
