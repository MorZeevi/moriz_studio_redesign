# Opening wordmark pattern

The owner rejected both the initial flowing bands and the subsequent rounded pixel clusters, requesting `moriz.studio` itself as the shape, then specifying a vertical direction and finally a single dramatically enlarged wordmark against the left edge. The SVG at `public/images/moriz-wordmark-pattern.svg` uses outlines from the site's Leon Heavy (`moriz.`) and Leon Regular (`studio`), preserving the header wordmark's proportions. One wordmark rotates 90 degrees in a tightly cropped viewBox and sits at the physical left edge, without repetition. The lettering is baked into vector paths, so the decorative mask does not depend on font loading. The alpha mask follows the palette without separate image variants or a runtime SVG filter.

`HomepagePattern` wraps the hero (including video and logos) and advantages inside main. Only its decorative background is hidden from assistive technology. The wrapper is positioned without creating a stacking context or clipping content: only the background clips at its bounds, ending exactly where Projects begins. The pattern sits at -2, beneath the existing page grain at -1. It does not extend behind the header, Projects or later sections. The global yellow backdrop retains its original -2 position and no longer needs pattern-specific ink rules.

- Mobile: one wordmark at least 160rem tall, shifting with 3% of local scroll distance.
- Desktop (1024px and above): one wordmark at least 240rem tall, shifting with 6% of local scroll distance.
- The mask preserves the lettering's proportions and fills at least the scope's height. Oversized lettering crops at the Projects boundary. The positive shift makes the background move slightly more slowly than the content; no repeat or tile wrap is needed.
- Light ink treatment: charcoal at 4% opacity; dark treatment: light at 3%. The background still follows Projects canvas ink while the end of the opening remains visible during that transition.
- Passive scrolling schedules one animation frame, with no idle loop. Work stops while the opening is offscreen or the document is hidden. Resize and font readiness remeasure the scope; unmount removes observers and listeners.
- Reduced motion and disabled JavaScript leave the single wordmark static within the same opening region. Theme fallback and native anchors remain functional.

`tests/navigation/homepage-pattern.spec.ts` checks desktop/mobile scrolling and reversal, accessible content, light/dark ink, exact Projects boundaries, absence below Projects, offscreen suspension, route cleanup, reduced motion, direct anchors and no-JavaScript behavior. It captures the hero, services, Projects boundary and lower canvas for visual review. Run it with `playwright.navigation.config.ts`, alongside hero video, opening, Projects backdrop and theme regressions.
