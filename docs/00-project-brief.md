## Project constraints

- The website will eventually support Hebrew and English.
- Hebrew is the primary language and the first version to plan and design.
- Hebrew pages must be treated as RTL.
- Keep information architecture language-independent where possible, with future English/LTR support in mind.
- Do not translate content or create English website copy yet.
- The owner has explicitly authorized a local coded black-and-white homepage wireframe after planning and research. Keep its layouts provisional for review.
- Draft 2–3 Hebrew content options per section from confirmed facts, refine the preferred version together and record agreed content/layout in the workbook. Do not invent evidence, write production website code, develop branding or treat prototype choices as final UX/design decisions on the owner's behalf.
- Use React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and Node.js for the wireframe implementation.
- The default page is Hebrew with `lang="he"` and `dir="rtl"`; all current UI text is Hebrew. Prepare English with `lang="en"` and `dir="ltr"` through shared components and separate dictionaries, without creating translations yet.
- The main navigation must include a language toggle. Hebrew is active now; English becomes available after its content is supplied.
- Use direction-aware CSS and layout, including logical spacing/positioning and start alignment. Final language URLs and multilingual SEO: TODO: Decision required
- Research precedes page hierarchy and visual design.
- Begin with a black-and-white homepage wireframe before developing and applying the visual identity. The project owner is experiencing a blocker and has chosen this starting point to help move forward.
- The preferred initial timebox is no more than one week, followed by ongoing additions and improvements.
- The first-week milestone is a developed homepage together with entirely new branding.
- The first-milestone branding scope includes fonts, typography, colors, a logo, and graphic elements.
- After that milestone, continue with About, Projects, individual Project / Case Study pages, the articles and research area, and the Diagnostic Questionnaire page.
- The Diagnostic Questionnaire is intended for a visual redesign only.
